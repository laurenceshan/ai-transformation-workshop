# Industry Scenario Library — Template

Copy this file as `industry-<name>.md` and fill it in to add a new industry.
Keep each scenario concrete enough to score with the rubric in
`scoring-rubric.md`. All content in English; the facilitator translates
during the interview as needed.

---

## Industry: <name>

### Industry Snapshot

- Typical business models and revenue drivers
- Where margin is made and lost
- Common systems (ERP/POS/CRM equivalents) and typical data maturity
- Regulatory or compliance constraints that affect AI use cases

### Value Chain Pain Map

For each link, list the 2-3 most common, most costly pain points:

- Demand / marketing / sales
- Core operations / fulfillment / service delivery
- Customer service / after-sales
- Back office (finance, HR, procurement, compliance)
- Management / reporting / knowledge

### Candidate Use Cases

Table of 8-15 candidates. Columns:

| Use case | Definition (who + what + outcome) | Typical value driver | Typical feasibility concern |
|---|---|---|---|

Guidelines:

- Mix quick wins (usually document/drafting/classification work) with
  structural bets (demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, agentic workflows).
- For each candidate, note the value driver in measurable terms (hours,
  error rate, conversion, inventory turns).
- Flag candidates that are commonly oversold — where vendor demos look
  great but real-world data or organization usually blocks them.

### Common Traps

- 2-4 failure patterns seen repeatedly in this industry (e.g. "chatbots
  deployed before the knowledge base exists").
